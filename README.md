## 🏛️ Design & Architecture

1. **Editorial Hero Section**: Full-bleed hero banner matching the layout of Page Law with sharp rectangular call-to-actions, direct contact number, and executive badges.
2. **Nigerian Jurisprudence Marquee**: Continuous ticker featuring the 7 pillars of Nigerian Law (The 1999 Constitution, National Assembly Acts, State Laws, Judicial Precedents, Customary Law, Sharia, and Received English Law).
3. **Structured Practice Areas**: 6 high-contrast practice cards with statutory citations (CAMA 2020, Arbitration & Mediation Act 2023, NDPA 2023, Land Use Act, Copyright Act 2022).
4. **Career Timeline**: Detailed chronology of legal internships and student leadership positions.
5. **Research & Awards**: Showcase of essay prizes and academic honors.
6. **The Law Verification Framework**: 4-pillar methodology (Authority-Backed Research, Template Fidelity, Audited Facts, Statutory Limitation Audits).
7. **Consultation & Retainer Dispatch**: Interactive form and direct links to phone, email, LinkedIn, and X.
